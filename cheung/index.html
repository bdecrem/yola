About

Lawrence Cheung is a Hongkonger who speaks Cantonese, English and Mandarin. Before joining the Department, he worked on a research study using a machine learning approach to computational parsing in English and Chinese.

Research

His areas of research are syntax, semantics, corpus linguistics and natural language processing (NLP). He has worked on grammatical phenomena across Cantonese, Mandarin and English. Specific topics include right dislocation structures, wh-placeholders, negative wh-construction, sentence-final particles, telicity marking, egophoricity and statistical analysis of grammatical variations.

He was engaged in a research corpus approach to grammar, automatic linguistic annotation, and syntactic parsing using machine learning. In recent years he also took part in interdisciplinary projects applying linguistic analysis and AI to Alzheimer's disease and autism.
